Process of Lapping & Coating

Our systematic processing sequence guarantees that every scaife meets strict industrial polish tolerances.

01

Inspection & Scoring

Each scaife is registered on our portal with its unique serial number. We run surface profile scoring tests to measure the degree of warp, groove depth, and runout.

02

Surface Lapping

The scaife is mounted on a CNC lapping table. We grind the surface using micro-abrasive slurries until the face is perfectly flat, verified via optical flats.

03

Chemical Coating

After polishing, plates are thoroughly degreased. We apply a thin, uniform chemical micro-layer (Standard, Premium, or DLC) to create keyways for diamond polishing grains.

04

Quality Testing & Delivery

The scaife goes through spin balancing and thickness checks. Once approved by the Quality Control team, it is packaged safely and dispatched to the client.
