Scaife Processing Services

High-precision surface finishing and metallurgical coating solutions designed exclusively for the diamond manufacturing industry.

Flatness Correction

Diamond Scaife Lapping

Over time, the friction of polishing diamonds creates grooves, ridges, and heat scores on the surface of cast iron scaifes. This uneven surface causes vibrations, leading to micro-cracks in diamonds and incorrect polishing facets.

Our machine lapping process grinds the plates to absolute flatness. We offer three tiers of precision lapping: Standard, Precision, and Ultra-Precision depending on your manufacturing requirements.

Key Benefits:

Restores sub-micron flatness
Eliminates spindle vibrations
Ensures perfect facet angles
Maximizes polished yield rate
Process: Cleaning → Runout Measurement → CNC Grinding with micro-abrasives → Verification via Optical flats → dynamic balancing check.
Adhesive Enhancement

Diamond Scaife Coating

A raw iron wheel cannot hold diamond powder. To allow polishing, scaifes must be coated with a micro-porous chemical layer that physically locks diamond grains onto the surface, creating an efficient cutting boundary.

We apply specialized microscopic coatings under controlled temperature conditions. From standard iron-oxide finishes to premium metal-carbide and advanced **Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC)** coatings, we provide surfaces tailored for different diamond sizes and types.

Key Benefits:

Superior abrasive grain retention
Extreme wear resistance (DLC)
Reduced operating temperatures
Multiplied coating service life
Process: Chemical solvent degreasing → Thermal pre-conditioning → Spray coating/Vacuum deposition → Micro-pore consolidation → Thickness audit.
Combined Maintenance

Lapping & Coating Bundle

For the best results, we recommend performing both lapping and coating together. Grinding the plate to perfect flatness before applying the coating ensures a uniform coating thickness across the entire diameter, avoiding soft spots and premature peeling.

This bundle represents our most popular service. You send us your worn scaife plates, and we return them fully flat, coated to your specifications, and dynamically balanced, ready for immediate installation.

Key Benefits:

Uniform coating distribution
Maximum adhesion interface
Discounted package pricing
Comprehensive QC clearance
Process: Full sequence of lapping followed immediately by our thermal coating and dynamic balancing processes.
Quality Control

Scaife Maintenance & Quality Inspection

Even if a plate doesn't need immediate coating, it may suffer from mechanical runout or balancing shifts due to high spinning velocities (often exceeding 3000 RPM). A minor imbalance can induce chatter marks on diamond facets.

We run scaifes through dynamic balance testing. Using high-frequency vibration sensors, we identify point weights and add balance adjustments. We also offer spindle runout auditing and micro-pore verification.

Key Benefits:

Prevents expensive facet chipping
Extends life of bench spindles
Verified certification documentation
Real-time vibration diagnosis
Process: Spindle mount test → High-frequency accelerometer profile → Point weight correction → Final QC certificate release.
