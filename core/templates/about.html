About Our Company

Dedicated to keeping the diamond cutting industry supplied with flat, balanced, and premium-coated scaifes since 2011.

Our Journey & History

Founded in the heart of Surat's diamond polishing cluster, our business began as a small workshops servicing local polishing benches. We realized early on that diamond cutters faced massive challenges with scaife wear, plate balancing, and coating adhesion, leading to uneven facets and damaged stones.

By investing in advanced CNC lapping machines and chemical vapor deposition research, we introduced precision-controlled lapping and high-tech DLC (Diamond-Like Carbon) coatings to the local market. Today, we operate two state-of-the-art service centers in Surat and Mumbai, processing thousands of scaifes annually for leading diamond manufacturers.

Our commitment to precision engineering remains unchanged. Every plate we process is measured to sub-micron tolerances, ensuring your polishing line operates efficiently without interruptions.

Why Customers Trust Us

  • Certified Flatness: We verify all plate profiles using high-accuracy dial indicators and optical tests.
  • Specialized Chemistry: Our coatings are formulated for extreme adhesion, keeping abrasive diamond powder secure.
  • Transparent Tracking: Customers can track progress, view inspection logs, and download service bills directly.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark for diamond processing plate technology, continually engineering durable and balanced surfaces that empower cutters to create perfect diamonds.

Our Mission

To provide high-precision scaife lapping and coating services using automated technology, ensuring minimal downtime, cost-efficient operations, and absolute service transparency for diamond manufacturers.

Core Values

Integrity in measurement, precision in execution, customer-centric support, and constant technological innovation in abrasive metallurgy.
